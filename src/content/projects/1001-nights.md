1001 Nights is an AI-native narrative game developed with Ada Eden.  

"The limits of my language mean the limits of my world"  
This is a game about storytelling.  
Inspired by the folktale collection "1001 Nights", this game follows the story of an evil king who, betrayed by his wife, marries a new maiden each day only to execute her the following morning. You, as Shahrzad, become his next bride. You possess the secret magic to turn language into reality... Can you end his reign of terror?

《1001 Nights》是 Ada Eden 开发的AI 原生叙事游戏。

这是一个由AI语言模型驱动的游戏，其中故事的力量塑造了现实...

改编自波斯民间传说：生性暴虐的国王山路亚尔怀疑遭到妻子背叛，遂每日迎娶新娘，次日清晨便将其处决。在游戏中，你将扮演山鲁佐德，成为他的下一位新娘……

通过讲述故事，你能否改变世界的命运？

Presented at Gamescom, the project is documented in the CHI EA 2025 paper *I Like Your Story!: A Co-Creative Story-Crafting Game with a Persona-Driven Character Based on Generative AI*.

该作品在 Gamescom 亮相，相关研究发表于 CHI EA 2025 论文 *I Like Your Story!: A Co-Creative Story-Crafting Game with a Persona-Driven Character Based on Generative AI*。
