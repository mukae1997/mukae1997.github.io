*Swords of West River* is an interactive performance created for the National Grand Theatre's "70 Years of Chinese Dance Art" exhibition. A dancer's movements are tracked in real time and translated into dynamic visual projections, blending traditional dance with responsive digital imagery.

*西河剑器* 是国家大剧院《一同走过——新中国舞蹈艺术 70 年》展览交互节目，由舞蹈演员 *唐诗逸* 表演。
