*Dayin Heming* (大音和鸣) is a music-themed interactive exhibition featuring two AI-produced installations.

**"Bai Niao Chao Feng"** — an AIGC production pipeline combining AI image generation → bird flight animation → sequence frame conversion → automated Sprite/Prefab import, delivered as an interactive wall where AI-generated birds converge on a phoenix.

**"Pi Pa Xing"** — transforming interaction design documents into AI image generation prompt packs, covering visual asset generation, outpainting, Unity engine import, and a touch-to-reveal experience on an ink-wash painting.

Throughout both installations, Unity Editor tooling paired with AI assistance rapidly pushed thin proposals into stable, exhibition-ready interactive products.

“大音和鸣”是一个以中国传统音乐文化为主题的沉浸式互动展览项目，通过 数字影像、互动装置与空间叙事，将古 典音乐意境转化为可观看、可进入、可 参与的现场体验。

项目围绕不同音乐主题设计了多组互动内容，观众可以在展厅中通过触发、观看与游走，感受声音、画面与空间彼此呼应的氛围，让传统文化以更轻盈、直观的方式被重新感知。
