*Echuu* turns virtual characters into livestream-ready, monetizable AI VTubers — users upload a 3D model, configure a persona, and the character auto-streams with real-time interaction, broadcast to YouTube, Twitch, and more.

Echuu 将虚拟角色转化为可直播、可变现的 AI VTuber Web 平台。用户上传 3D 角色模型、配置角色设定后即可自动直播互动，并可推流至 YouTube / Twitch 等。
