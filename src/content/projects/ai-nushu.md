*AI Nüshu* is an interactive art installation exploring AI-driven language emergence through the lens of Nüshu script — a historical writing system created and used exclusively by women. Two AI agents learn from a Chinese dictionary and Nüshu corpus, then develop their own writing system through observation and mutual communication.

AI Nüshu 是一个受女书启发而形成的涌现式语言系统。作品以互动装置的形式展开，训练两个 AI 智能体学习中文词典与女书语料，并通过持续观察环境与彼此交流，共同发展出一套用于编码中文的书写系统。

An artistic interpretation of how a non-Western writing system might be generated, bringing together AI, Chinese cultural heritage, and a feminist perspective.

从计算语言学视角，对一种非西方文字系统的生成方式进行了艺术化诠释，同时将 AI 技术、中国文化遗产与女性主义视角结合在一起。
