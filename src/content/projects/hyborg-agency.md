*Hyborg (Hybrid+Agent)* is a form of intelligent life, as a metaphor for non-human AI agents in the wild. In the future, electronics and data mutated to creatures that can communicate with human, with their personas and memories. 

Hyborg（Hybrid+Agent）是一种智能生命形式——关于非人类 AI 智能体的隐喻。在未来，电子设备和数据会突变为能够与人类沟通的生物，拥有它们的人格与记忆。

This project explores whether communication within human groups can nourish non-human AI agents into social members. Based on AI chatbots in human communities in Discord, this project aims to explore the Human-AI relationship in human society.

本项目探索人类社群中的交流是否能将非人类 AI 智能体培育为社会成员。基于部署在 Discord 社区中的 AI 聊天机器人，旨在审视 AI 在人类社会中的关系定位。

It is also an official showcase project selected by the Needle framework team ([live demo](https://engine.needle.tools/samples/hyborg-agency/?room=needle479)).

该项目也被 Needle 框架团队选为官方 Showcase（[在线演示](https://engine.needle.tools/samples/hyborg-agency/?room=needle479)）。
