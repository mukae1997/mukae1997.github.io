*Humans, Space, Time, and People* is an interactive digital installation presented at Beijing Contemporary Art Expo 2021. A camera captures visitors' faces in real time, feeding a generative visual system that transforms each person into a living element of the composition.

北京当代艺博会 2021 交互数字装置。设计整体系统架构，实现 Unity 视觉效果，包含实时人脸检测与生成式内容系统。
