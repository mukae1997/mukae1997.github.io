*Autumn Wind in Nanyuan* is a multimedia interactive dance drama produced by China National Song and Dance Theater. 


The production blends Notch real-time visuals with Unity-based stage control, synchronizing Hecoos, Unity, and Notch over OSC for a seamless multi-platform performance.

中国东方歌舞团出品多媒体交互舞剧。

设计完整技术方案，制作 Notch 视觉特效，实现 Unity 中控系统，统一协调 Hecoos / Unity / Notch 的 OSC 同步。
